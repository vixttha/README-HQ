# Explain where project decisions are made

**Use when.** Community proposals and final decisions happen in different places.

**Reader task.** Handoff and maintenance.

**Practice.** Link a project’s governance and identify who makes major decisions. Give proposals a route when that route differs from submitting a patch. Kubernetes’s steering README describes its decision-making scope and links its charter.

**Example (illustrative).** Point design proposals to the discussion area and identify where decisions are recorded.

**Result to check.** A reader can find where to start a proposal and where to find its decision.

**Evidence.** Research reference. Run the reader check on the target project before relying on the documented outcome.

**Research references.** [Pinned Kubernetes steering README](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/e1963fc0dd9debd04690163f50a6178e278b9779/committee-steering/README.md#L9-L15)
