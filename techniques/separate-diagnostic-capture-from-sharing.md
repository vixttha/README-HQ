# Separate diagnostic capture from sharing

**Use when.** Support may ask for logs or a bundle containing local details.

**Reader task.** First result and recovery.

**Practice.** Tell readers how to capture relevant evidence, where the resulting bundle lands, and how to inspect or anonymize it before sending it through the support route. Docker distinguishes diagnostic capture and upload. Atlassian documents support-bundle anonymization.

**Example (illustrative).** Generate a local bundle, review filenames and redact identifiers, then upload through support.

**Result to check.** A tester can produce and inspect the bundle before any transfer.

**Evidence.** Research reference. Run the reader check on the target project before relying on the documented outcome.

**Research references.** [Docker Desktop diagnostics](https://docs.docker.com/desktop/troubleshoot-and-support/troubleshoot/) · [Atlassian support-bundle guidance](https://support.atlassian.com/jira/kb/anonymize-jira-support-zip-data/)
