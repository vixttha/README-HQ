# Benchmark method and scope

**Use when.** A performance number would be misleading without its workload or conditions.

**Reader task.** Verification and trust.

**Practice.** Describe the workload, environment, revision, and units beside a performance result.

**Example (illustrative).** Record the dataset, hardware, revisions, command, and units next to the latency comparison.

**Result to check.** A reader can rerun the workload and interpret the reported values.

**Evidence.** Illustrative practice. Add a project example or test result when this technique is evaluated.
