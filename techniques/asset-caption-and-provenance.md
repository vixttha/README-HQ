# Asset caption and provenance

**Use when.** A visual uses external work or needs version or authorship context.

**Reader task.** Demos and visuals.

**Practice.** Name what a visual shows and identify its source or build where relevant.

**Example (illustrative).** Caption a map with the dataset version, creator or provider, and reuse license.

**Result to check.** A reader can follow the credit to the source and understand what the image shows.

**Evidence.** Illustrative practice. Add a project example or test result when this technique is evaluated.
