# Use labeled callouts at the point of action

**Use when.** A warning or note only matters for one specific action.

**Reader task.** Navigation and accessibility.

**Practice.** Use short labeled notices for important context or consequences beside the relevant step. Keep essential information readable in plain text when the renderer handles callouts differently.

**Example (illustrative).** Put a data-removal warning directly above the reset step it qualifies.

**Result to check.** Readers see the consequence before they reach the affected command.

**Evidence.** Research reference. Run the reader check on the target project before relying on the documented outcome.
