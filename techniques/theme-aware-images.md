**Use when.** The same image loses contrast in one of the supported color themes.

**Reader task.** Demos and visuals.

**Practice.** Check readability against light and dark backgrounds and provide variants when needed.

**Example (illustrative).** Offer light and dark product diagrams with the same labels and an accessible fallback.

**Result to check.** Open the rendered README in both themes and check contrast and fallback text.
