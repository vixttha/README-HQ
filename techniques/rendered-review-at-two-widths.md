# Rendered review at two widths

**Use when.** Wide tables, screenshots, or code blocks may be hard to use on narrow screens.

**Reader task.** Verification and trust.

**Practice.** Check the landing page on wide and narrow layouts for overflow or unreadable content.

**Example (illustrative).** Open the rendered README at narrow and wide widths and inspect its diagram and table.

**Result to check.** The important content remains legible and usable at both stated widths.

**Evidence.** Illustrative practice. Add a project example or test result when this technique is evaluated.
