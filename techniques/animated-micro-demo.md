**Use when.** The order of a short sequence is important to understanding it.

**Reader task.** Demos and visuals.

**Practice.** Show a short interaction sequence that needs no narration.

**Example (illustrative).** Show a three-step clip of selecting a file, running the formatter, and inspecting the resulting diff.

**Result to check.** A reader can understand the sequence on one playback and via its text summary.
