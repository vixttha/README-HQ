# Match firmware to the physical hardware revision

**Use when.** Firmware files look interchangeable and hardware compatibility differs.

**Reader task.** Quickstart and adoption.

**Practice.** Help readers identify the device revision and map it to the correct build or download target. QMK distinguishes buildable keyboard revisions and documents target selection.

**Example (illustrative).** Map rev-A and rev-B boards to their corresponding firmware filenames and published digests.

**Result to check.** A reader can identify their board and select its supported firmware before flashing.

**Evidence.** Research reference. Run the reader check on the target project before relying on the documented outcome.

**Research references.** [QMK keyboard guidelines](https://docs.qmk.fm/hardware_keyboard_guidelines) · [QMK target selection](https://docs.qmk.fm/getting_started_make_guide)
