# Give color-coded information a text label

**Use when.** A screenshot, table, or chart uses color to convey meaning.

**Reader task.** Navigation and accessibility.

**Practice.** Label states, branches, or series in words when color conveys their meaning. Readers should still understand a status table or diagram with the colors removed.

**Example (illustrative).** Use a text label such as “Passed” beside the green result indicator.

**Result to check.** Meaning is recoverable in monochrome and through the document’s text.

**Evidence.** Research reference. Run the reader check on the target project before relying on the documented outcome.

**Research references.** [W3C guidance on use of color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html)
