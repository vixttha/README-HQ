# Recovery beside the failed step

**Use when.** A common failure occurs at a specific command or prerequisite.

**Reader task.** First result and recovery.

**Practice.** Put a common failure and its recovery close to the command or action that produces it.

**Example (illustrative).** Under the connect command, explain what a connection refusal means and link the startup check.

**Result to check.** An intentionally skipped prerequisite produces the stated symptom and the recovery works.

**Evidence.** Illustrative practice. Add a project example or test result when this technique is evaluated.
