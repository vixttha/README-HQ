# Next action after success

**Use when.** The quickstart ends before a common next task.

**Reader task.** First result and recovery.

**Practice.** Offer a natural second task once the first one works.

**Example (illustrative).** After a successful local export, link to the guide for supplying a custom schema.

**Result to check.** A reader knows the next task and reaches its first step from the result.
